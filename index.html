Scheduling rules

How the planner fills your calendar.

Follow task order
The first unfinished task gets the next available session. New tasks are added to the end.
Roll unfinished work forward
Missed sessions do not count as completed. The next available session remains assigned to the unfinished task.
Protect your break
Any configured time inside your break is shifted to the break end plus your chosen offset. Duplicate times are merged.
